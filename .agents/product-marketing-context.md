# Product Marketing Context (shared)

Last updated: 2026-11-02. Built from Google Drive strategy docs, Fireflies demo transcripts, and internal planning memos. Cite the sources in parentheses when carrying claims into downstream work.

## Product

- **What we sell:** MPP (MAP Policy Partners) is a cloud-based SaaS platform that automates Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) policy monitoring and enforcement for CPG manufacturers, brands, and agencies. It crawls online marketplaces to find authorized and unauthorized sellers advertising below the brand's minimum price, then runs a configurable three-strike enforcement workflow (warning email, cease-and-desist, certified letter / legal escalation) with timestamped screenshot evidence. (GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03; MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
- **Category we claim:** MAP monitoring and enforcement software. Positioned as a focused, niche solution, not a general e-commerce analytics suite or a broad brand-protection platform. Adjacent markets MPP is considering extending into: broader price intelligence / competitive benchmarking, international MAP, GEO/AI-shopping monitoring, and retailer-facing compliance dashboards. (GDrive: DP's MPP Overview, 2026-08-25)
- **Primary value:** Enterprise-grade MAP monitoring with fast onboarding (hours, not weeks), full feature access at every tier (no feature gating), flexible usage-based pricing, and strong hands-on support, at a price point accessible to mid-market brands who would otherwise monitor manually. Customers say MPP has "the same capabilities" as higher-priced competitors and that power users can "run the entire program from the inbox." (GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03)
- **Key features that matter to buyers:**
  - Monitoring dashboard with real-time violation counts, top violators by product and seller, and MAP trend lines (GDrive: MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
  - Daily email summaries (violations + unauthorized sellers) (GDrive: MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
  - Seller identification database: emails resolved for roughly 80% of listings (GDrive: MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
  - Sole-violator detection, flagging "race to the bottom" starts (GDrive: MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
  - Automated three-strike enforcement with templated emails, certified letters, and screenshot/timestamp evidence (GDrive: MPP Overview; MPP Product Overview)
  - Product groups + discount scheduling so legitimate promos don't trigger enforcement (GDrive: MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
  - Custom reports, white-label-ready exports for agencies, SKU-level stop-ship rules (Fireflies: Boehringer Ingelheim <> MAP Policy Partners Demo, 2026-10-15; Boehringer Ingelheim: MAP Policy Partners Follow Up, 2026-10-30)
  - Look-back period workflow and 30-day historical trends; up to 36-month historical/custom reporting (Fireflies: Boehringer Ingelheim Follow Up, 2026-10-30)
  - Monitoring across Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Google Shopping, Chewy, and retail sites, incl. prices behind logins or in carts (Fireflies: Resideo Demo, 2026-09-29; Petcurean Demo, 2026-08-12)

## Market

- **Primary ICP:** Mid-market CPG manufacturers and brands with 100 to a few thousand SKUs distributed across many online retailers and marketplaces. Verticals showing up in live demos and the strategy brief include: pet / animal health (Petcurean, Boehringer Ingelheim animal health), connected home / safety (Resideo), tires and auto (Discount Tire), consumer electronics (President Electronics), home furnishings (Tov Furniture), small housewares (Bunn), musical instruments / pro audio, prestige beauty, specialty footwear, supplements and wellness, firearms and hunting, safety and industrial gear. (Fireflies: Petcurean Demo 2026-08-12, Resideo Demo 2026-09-29, Boehringer Ingelheim Demo 2026-10-15; GDrive: DP's MPP Overview, 2026-08-25)
- **Secondary segments:**
  - Digital marketing / e-commerce agencies that resell MAP monitoring as a service and need white-labeled reporting (GDrive: MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
  - Enterprise brands with large SKU footprints (4,000+) and complex two-step distribution; these show up but stress scalability and single-operator manageability concerns (Fireflies: Resideo Demo, 2026-09-29)
  - International brands (Canada especially, given Section 76 Competition Act changes effective June 2025) (GDrive: DP's MPP Overview, 2026-08-25)
- **Buyer roles / buying committee:** Multi-stakeholder, typically brand / channel-management lead as champion plus procurement, legal/compliance, and e-commerce ops. Observed roles on demos: Director/Senior Manager of Trade Compliance, Brand Protection Manager, E-commerce Manager, Digital Channel Lead, Procurement/Legal reviewer for NDA and pricing. (Fireflies: Boehringer Ingelheim Demo, 2026-10-15; Resideo Demo, 2026-09-29; Petcurean Demo, 2026-08-12)

## Competitive

- **Top competitors / alternatives:**
  - Brand Alignment (BA) is the most referenced direct competitor; MPP has done side-by-side pricing comparisons with them, scoped to MAP monitoring only (GDrive: Session 4 Working Doc: Pricing Comparison, BA vs. MPP, 2026-08-24)
  - "Broader e-commerce analytics / brand protection" suites that bundle MAP into bigger packages with expensive services (unnamed in Overview; GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03)
  - Status-quo alternative: manual, complaint-driven monitoring. Prospects describe sending enforcement emails by hand, reviewing recurring violations in spreadsheets, and relying on reactive distributor complaints. (Fireflies: Resideo Demo 2026-09-29; Boehringer Ingelheim Demo 2026-10-15)
  - Petcurean mentioned "gaps in their existing MAP tools" as the reason to shop MPP, implying a prior point tool was in place (Fireflies: Petcurean Demo, 2026-08-12). Specific incumbent name TBD -- worth tagging in future win/loss notes.
- **How we usually differ from them:**
  - Full feature access at every tier, no feature gating. Price scales with usage (SKUs, scan frequency, marketplaces, regions), not with capability. (GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03)
  - Fast, turnkey onboarding (hours), minimal technical integration, training and support bundled in subscription (GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03)
  - Monthly plans accessible from the mid-market up ($150/month low end to $10K/month high end quoted in the Overview; recent live quotes land near $2,500/mo for ~350 SKUs on daily scans and $3,100/mo for twice-daily, 40-42 merchants) (GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03; Fireflies: Boehringer Ingelheim Follow Up, 2026-10-30)
  - Enforcement engine is the most-differentiated piece: three-strike workflow, certified-letter generator, evidence capture. Customers say "MPP nails our enforcement process." (GDrive: MPP Product Overview, 2025-08-11)
  - Flexible contract terms, including month-to-month volume increases/decreases (GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03)

## Go-to-market

- **Current motion (PLG, sales-led, hybrid):** Sales-led, demo-driven. Matt Ross is organizing demos directly with prospect buying committees, followed by follow-up sessions, NDAs, pricing tiers, case studies, and a one-week trial offer on a subset of SKUs. (Fireflies: Boehringer Ingelheim Demo 2026-10-15; Resideo Demo 2026-09-29; Petcurean Demo 2026-08-12). Inbound funnel is heavily supported by Google Ads: a single "MAP Policy Partners Leads" campaign across six MAP-focused ad groups has spent ~$152K lifetime for 434 conversions at ~$350 blended CPA; since Beacon acquisition (2025-07-14), 67 conversions on $21.5K spend at ~$321 CPA, feeding 36 demos and 9 conversions across Google Ads, SEO, and outbound email. (GDrive: MPP GTM Overview, 2026-03-31)
- **Pricing model at a glance:** Usage-based SaaS. Four multiplicative levers: SKUs monitored, scan frequency per day, number of marketplaces, regions monitored. Monthly plans range from ~$150 to ~$10K; targeted new tier floor at $250/month for 200 SKUs. Pricing framework is being standardized (Stripe + DocuSign automation underway). Target gross margin ~68% implying ~$0.011/scrape against ~$0.008 cost; Discount Tire modelled near $300K ARR. (GDrive: MPP Overview, 2025-08-03; [MPP] Pricing Strategy session, 2026-04-27; MPP: Discount Tire Pricing Working Session, 2026-05-21; Session 4 Working Doc: Pricing Comparison, BA vs. MPP, 2026-08-24)
